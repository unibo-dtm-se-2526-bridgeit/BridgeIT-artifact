Guide

How BridgeIT works

No technical background needed — four short steps take a requirement from a plain-language idea to something IT can build with confidence.

  1. 1

    Write it down

    Go to Create and describe what you need, in your own words — no special format required. It's saved with status Submitted.

  2. 2

    Get a quick check from Gemini

    Copy the id you were given, paste it into Analyse, and ask for a quality check. Gemini either confirms it's ready, or tells you what's missing and why it matters — a first pass to help you improve it, never a verdict.

  3. 3

    A Business Analyst reviews it

    On Validate, using that same id, a Business Analyst makes the real decision: Approve it as-is, Edit the text and send it back for another check, or Reject it.

  4. 4

    Check on it anytime

    Paste the id into Requirements whenever you want to see its current text and status.
